# Console

The console at [console.lightpanda.io](https://console.lightpanda.io) is where you manage your Lightpanda Cloud account without touching the API. You use it to create tokens, watch your usage, review past sessions, and handle billing. See [Getting started](/run-on-lightpanda-cloud/getting-started) to create your account and first token.

## What you can manage

**API tokens.** Your token authenticates every request, whether you connect over [CDP](/run-on-lightpanda-cloud/getting-started), [MCP](/usage/mcp), or the [HTTP API](/usage/api). From the console you can create tokens, name them, and revoke one you no longer trust. Use separate tokens for separate apps so you can revoke one without breaking the others.

<Callout type="warning">
A token is shown once, when you create it. Save it somewhere safe. If you lose it, revoke it and create a new one.
</Callout>

**Sessions and logs.** Every browser you run on the cloud is a session. The console lists your recent sessions and lets you open one to inspect what happened, including its container logs. Reach for this when a script behaves differently on the cloud than it did locally, or when a session ends earlier than you expect.

**Usage and billing.** The console tracks your usage against your plan: the current billing period, live activity, and history. Watch it to see how close you are to your monthly browser hours and your concurrency limit. The billing page shows your plan and lets you upgrade, downgrade, or open the Stripe customer portal to manage your payment method and invoices. See [Limits and billing](/run-on-lightpanda-cloud/limits-and-billing) for the plans and how usage is charged.

**Proxies.** You can route your cloud traffic through your own proxy. Add and manage your proxies from the console, then select one per session. See [Configure a proxy](/guides/configure-a-proxy) for the connection details.
