# Limits and billing

Lightpanda Cloud bills by browser time. Each plan includes a monthly pool of browser hours and a cap on how many sessions you can run at once. You start on the free plan, and you only pay when you need more hours or more concurrency.

## Plans

Three plans cover the range from testing to production. Every plan lets you bring your own proxy.

- **Explorer** is free, with a browser-hour pool and concurrency limit sized for testing, and community support on Discord.
- **Builder** is for production workloads: a larger monthly pool of browser hours and concurrent sessions, billed at a per-hour rate once you go over the included hours, plus email and Slack support.
- **Enterprise** is custom volume, concurrency, and support, with an SLA. For high-volume needs, on-premise, or a private cloud, [contact us](mailto:hello@lightpanda.io).

## How usage is measured

Two limits apply to every plan: how many browser hours you use in a billing period, and how many sessions you run at the same time.

**Browser hours** are the total time your sessions stay open, metered by the second and summed over your billing period, so short sessions cost little. This is the number that counts against your monthly pool and, on paid plans, drives the overage rate.

**Concurrent sessions** are the sessions open at one instant. This limit is about parallelism, not total volume: a plan's concurrency cap still lets you run many sessions a day, as long as no more than that cap overlap at once.

## When you reach a limit

If you open more sessions than your concurrency limit allows, the extra sessions are rejected with HTTP `429 Too Many Requests`. Retry once a running session ends, or upgrade for more concurrency.

When you use up your monthly browser hours, the behavior depends on your plan. The free plan stops until its monthly pool resets. Paid plans keep running and bill the overage at the per-hour rate shown on the [pricing page](https://lightpanda.io/pricing).

## Manage your subscription

You manage billing from the [console](https://console.lightpanda.io). The billing page shows your current plan and usage, lets you upgrade to Builder, and opens the Stripe customer portal where you can update your payment method, download invoices, and cancel.
